Who I am

About Me

I'm a final year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Christ College of Engineering, Irinjalakuda, drawn to the point where hardware and software meet — embedded systems, IoT and robotics.

My work so far spans microcontroller programming on platforms like Arduino, ESP32, STM32 and PIC16F877A, interfacing sensors, and building small automation and safety systems from the ground up. Internships at IIIT Kottayam, BSNL RTTC and Evolve Robotics have given me hands-on time with IoT device-to-cloud pipelines, telecom infrastructure and embedded C firmware respectively.

I'm particularly interested in hardware-software integration and biomedical electronics — reflected in projects like a muscle-signal translation device for paralyzed individuals and a smart reminder system for patients, alongside more classic embedded safety builds like an alcohol-detection engine lock. I enjoy the full loop: wiring a circuit, writing the firmware, and watching it respond to the physical world in real time.
